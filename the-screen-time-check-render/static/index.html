What it does

One question, answered honestly, in ninety seconds

It checks your project against eight markers of static thinking, all of them from OneRevel's own published work. You get one screen back: how many are present, the single one costing you the most, and what to do about that one before your next meeting.

Where this came from. The ebook ends with one offer from Brett Unzicker: even a brief review of early documentation can identify risk before it becomes cost.

This is that review, run by you, in ninety seconds. If it finds something, the conversation is the next step rather than the first one.

What it will not do

  • It will not price anything. No figures, no savings claim, no quote.
  • It will not tell you your technology is wrong. It is about the document, not the brands in it.
  • It will not say your consultant was wrong. It asks who was in the room and lets that answer speak.
